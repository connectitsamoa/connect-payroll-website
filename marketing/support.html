Connect Payroll · Support

Help that keeps payroll moving.

Use the route that matches your question. We will ask for the minimum information needed and will never ask you to email a password, login code, bank account number or full payslip.

Support guidance reviewed: 19 August 2026

Contact routes

support@connectpayroll.wsSetup help, access questions, display issues and normal product support.
sales@connectpayroll.wsNew business enquiries, walkthroughs and quotations.
payroll@connectpayroll.wsAutomated invitations, login codes and approved wage-statement delivery. It is not a general support inbox.
Urgent security concernContact support@connectpayroll.ws immediately for a wrong recipient, suspected exposure, lost device or compromised account.

What to include

  • Your business name and a safe callback route.
  • The approximate time of the issue and whether payday is affected.
  • The affected screen, pay-run status or non-sensitive error message.
  • A redacted screenshot with employee names, bank details, statutory numbers and access codes removed.

How we handle a problem

We first confirm whether the issue affects one employee, one business or all customers. We preserve the relevant run and audit evidence, avoid changing a paid run or resending a sensitive document while the cause is uncertain, and record the resolution. The business’s established spreadsheet and payment process remain the authority if a payroll cannot yet be trusted.

Connect IT does not publish a guaranteed response-time promise on this page. Any service levels or support hours are confirmed in the customer agreement.
