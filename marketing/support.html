Use the route that matches your question. We will ask for the minimum information needed and will never ask you to email a password, login code, bank account number or full payslip.

What to include

  • Your business name and a safe callback route.
  • The approximate time of the issue and whether payday is affected.
  • The affected screen, pay-run status or non-sensitive error message.
  • A redacted screenshot with employee names, bank details, statutory numbers and access codes removed.

How we handle a problem

We first check whether the problem affects one employee, one business or the whole service. We keep the original payroll record safe, avoid changing a paid run while the cause is uncertain, and record how the issue was resolved.

If a problem could affect someone’s pay, pause payment and contact us before continuing. Any agreed support hours or response times are listed in your customer agreement.
