Common questions

Good to know before you start.

If your business works differently, talk to us. Payroll should fit the business—not the other way around.

Do I need to be a payroll expert?

No. The setup explains each field in plain language, and our team can help you with the first run.

Do employees need to clock in?

No. You can run payroll completely by hand. Attendance can be added later if your business needs it.

Can we pay weekly, fortnightly or monthly?

Yes. You choose the schedule and the day each pay period begins.

Can one person run payroll?

Yes. Connect Payroll does not force a complicated approval chain. You can invite more payroll officers if needed.

Does it pay staff or file returns automatically?

No. It prepares payslips, payment registers and statutory reports for you to check and use.
