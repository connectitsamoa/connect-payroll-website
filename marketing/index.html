A straightforward payroll workflow

From people to pay in three calm steps.

Connect Payroll is designed for the person who actually runs payroll. No mandatory approval chain and no need to import attendance before you can begin.

Open Connect Payroll ↗
  1. 01

    Set up the business

    Enter business details, choose the pay schedule and add employees manually. Bank details and email are optional where cash or printed statements are used.

  2. 02

    Enter and review pay

    Load normal pay for fixed or salaried staff, then enter daily hours or period totals for hourly staff. Review exceptions and statutory calculations.

  3. 03

    Finish and share

    Finalise the run, download statements and registers, then record when the run is paid. Corrections stay linked to the original run.

+

Optional when you need it

Start with manual payroll even if your team does not use a biometric clock. When you are ready, ZKTeco and ZKBio Zlink attendance can be added as an input—not a requirement.
