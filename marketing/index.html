Aso totogi · How a pay week goes

From Monday’s first shift to fa’afetai on payday.

Here’s a normal pay week, start to finish. The screens are the real app, with sample staff.

  1. 01 Monday · 7:30am

    Set up your staff once.

    Put in each person’s pay rate, how often they’re paid, and whether it goes to the bank or in cash. Bus fares, allowances, social fund and loan repayments only need entering once. After that they come through every payday on their own.

    • Paid weekly, fortnightly or monthly
    • By the hour, a set amount or a salary
    • A short explanation next to every field

  2. 02 Friday · 5:00pm

    Put in the week’s hours.

    Type start and finish times, or copy a normal day across the week. Overtime, sick days, annual leave and public holidays are paid at the right rates. You don’t need a time clock, but if you have one, the timesheet and clock import add-on reads its exports.

    • The whole team on one screen
    • Leave days paid for you
    • Late minutes aren’t paid

  3. 03 Monday · 9:00am

    Check it before anyone’s paid.

    PAYE comes straight from Revenue’s weekly, fortnightly and monthly tables. NPF is 10% from the worker and 10% from you, and ACC is 1%. If something looks wrong, like missing hours or deductions that are too high, you’ll see it before you approve.

    • Fortnightly tax balanced across both weeks
    • A warning if deductions go over 35%
    • Nothing is final until you approve it

  4. 04 Wednesday · Aso Totogi

    Payday.

    Approve the pay run, then print or email the payslips and take the bank list to your bank. If you pay in cash, the cash sheet tells you which notes and coins go in each envelope, rounded up to the next 10 sene.

    • A payslip for every person
    • A bank list and a cash sheet
    • Every pay run saved, so you can look back any time

Questions

Common questions.

Do I need to know payroll?

No. If you can fill in a form, you can do payday. Every field has a short explanation, and the Home screen tells you what to do next.

Do my staff need a time clock?

No. You can type the hours in, or copy a normal day. If you already have a time clock, from ZKTeco, Hikvision, Dahua, Uniview, Anviz or another maker, the timesheet and clock import add-on (WST 30 a month) reads its exports. If yours isn’t read straight away, send us an export and we’ll add it at no extra cost.

Does it follow Samoa’s PAYE, NPF and ACC rules?

Yes. PAYE uses Revenue’s weekly, fortnightly and monthly tables. NPF is 10% from the worker and 10% from you, and ACC is 1%.

Can I pay some staff in cash?

Yes. The cash sheet shows which notes and coins to count for each person, rounded up to the next 10 sene.

Does it pay my staff or file returns for me?

No. You still pay through your bank or in cash, and you hand in the P4 and SNPF schedule yourself. Connect Payroll gets it all ready and printed.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Call or message us and we’ll walk you through it using your own business.
